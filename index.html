04 / THE THOUGHT BEHIND ITBUILT TO ENDURE
MATERIALS · ENGINEERING · DAILY COMFORT

How we build
shapes how you live

At Guru Status, reinforced-concrete walls and sound-insulated entrance doors help reduce noise. Double glazing helps keep indoor temperatures more comfortable, while layers of moisture protection help shield the building from Batumi’s humid coastal climate.

01
WALL CONSTRUCTION

Monolithic reinforced-concrete walls

Walls between apartments are monolithic reinforced concrete, while the exterior walls are also monolithic reinforced concrete to help maintain warmth in winter and coolness in summer.

02
WINDOW AESTHETICS

Matte-black double glazing

Instead of regular aluminum window frames, Guru Status uses matte-black frames with double glazing, significantly enhancing the building’s aesthetic character.

03
STAINED-GLASS FRAMES

Stainless aluminum framing

For stained-glass windows, stainless aluminum frames with double glazing are used. Unlike plastic, they are more resistant to the humid environment and are designed to last longer.

04
VERTICAL MOBILITY

KONE elevators

Elevator systems by KONE, an internationally recognized manufacturer chosen to support dependable daily movement throughout the residence.

05
ENTRANCE DOORS

High-quality door systems

Doors with a steel frame and wood texture, designed with reliable internal sound insulation, tightly fitting rubber corners for enhanced noise suppression, and solid door casings for additional protection.

06
EXTERIOR GLAZING

LAMEX facade glass

High-quality LAMEX glass is used for balconies and terraces. LAMEX is part of the Japanese KOKUYO Group and was selected for quality, finish and long-term confidence.

07
FACADE PROTECTION

Triple moisture protection

The facade includes a moisture-proof layer with multiple barriers, providing triple protection against dampness — an important consideration in a humid seaside setting.

08
AIR QUALITY

Special ventilation ducts

A newer technology for Batumi, the dedicated ventilation duct system is designed to eliminate unpleasant odors and create a more comfortable environment for residents.
